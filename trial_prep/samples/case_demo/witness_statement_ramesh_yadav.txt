STATEMENT UNDER SECTION 161 CrPC
Witness: Ramesh Yadav, S/o Mahesh Yadav, R/o 4 Gandhi Road, Rampur
Recorded by: SI Devendra Singh
Date: 15/11/2025

I am a shopkeeper with a stall next to Shree Traders on Gandhi Road. On
03/11/2025 at about 19:30 hrs, I was closing my stall when I saw two men
approach Arvind Mehta near the entrance of Shree Traders. I recognized one
of them as Suresh Kumar, who is known in the area over a dispute about
unpaid goods. The second man I did not recognize at the time.

Suresh Kumar was already carrying a wooden stick when they approached
Arvind. Within a few seconds of exchanging words, Suresh struck Arvind on
the head with the stick, and the second man joined in, hitting Arvind on
the arm. Arvind fell and tried to cover his head. I shouted for help and
ran over; the two men fled toward Bazaar Lane. I helped Arvind to a nearby
auto-rickshaw to go to the hospital.

I later saw the second man again on 14/11/2025 near Bazaar Lane and
recognized him as Manoj Tiwari, who runs a tea stall there.

Signature: Ramesh Yadav
