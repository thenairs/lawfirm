STATEMENT UNDER SECTION 161 CrPC
Witness: Arvind Mehta (Complainant), S/o Late Mohan Mehta, R/o 12 Nehru
Colony, Rampur
Recorded by: SI Devendra Singh
Date: 18/11/2025

On 03/11/2025 at about 19:00 hrs, I was standing outside Shree Traders
waiting to speak with the shop owner about a delivery. Suresh Kumar came
up to me and started arguing loudly about money he claims I owe him for
goods from October. At first it was only shouting and no weapon was
visible. The argument went on for some time before a second man I did not
know joined Suresh. It was only after the second man arrived that Suresh
picked up a wooden stick lying near the shop entrance and hit me on the
head. I was struck again on my left arm as I tried to block the blow. I do
not clearly remember how long the argument lasted before the stick was
used, but it felt like several minutes. A nearby shopkeeper, I believe
Ramesh, came to help me and the two men ran off.

Signature: Arvind Mehta
